# NutriNova AI Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-29

This draft is for product planning and legal review. It is not legal advice.

## What NutriNova AI Collects

NutriNova AI may collect account information, profile details, nutrition and meal logs, food preferences, allergies, body metrics, habit history, uploaded meal photos, barcode scans, custom foods, recipes, pantry records, grocery lists, and device/app diagnostics.

## Why Data Is Used

Data is used to provide wellness tracking, calculate nutrition summaries, show trends, support meal and habit logging, improve food matching, generate non-medical insights, maintain security, debug issues, and operate the service.

## Sensitive Wellness Data

Nutrition, weight, body metrics, allergies, and food photos can be sensitive. NutriNova AI should treat this data with strong access controls, secure transport, encrypted storage where appropriate, audit trails for administrative access, and strict user ownership checks.

## AI And Photo Analysis

AI photo analysis is an estimate. Photos are processed to identify possible foods and portions. The app should not save AI-detected foods as meal logs until the user confirms or corrects them.

## Sharing

User data should not be sold. Data should only be shared with infrastructure providers, analytics/error monitoring providers, food-data providers where required, or legal/compliance recipients when necessary. Any future family sharing or coach sharing should require explicit user action.

## Retention And Deletion

Users should be able to request data export and deletion. Backups may retain deleted data for a limited operational window before aging out.

## Security

Production deployments should use HTTPS, secure cookies, limited admin access, environment-managed secrets, structured logging without raw secrets, storage bucket access controls, and regular database backups.

## Children

NutriNova AI should not knowingly collect data from children without appropriate consent and compliance review.
