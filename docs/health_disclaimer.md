# NutriNova AI Health Disclaimer

NutriNova AI is for wellness tracking, education, and personal organization only.

It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Nutrition values, calorie targets, macro targets, AI photo estimates, barcode data, and insights may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Always confirm food identity and portion size before saving a meal. AI photo nutrition is an estimate and should be reviewed by the user.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making major changes to diet, exercise, supplements, medication, or weight-management plans, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, have a history of disordered eating, or are managing allergies or chronic disease.

In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
